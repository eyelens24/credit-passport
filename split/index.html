Credit Passport

    Lenders can't price risk they can't see.

    When someone's history is overseas, or their rent goes to a housemate instead of an agent, lenders can't check it. So the applicant gets treated as higher risk, or their file waits in manual review.

    Unverified isn't the same as risky. Credit Passport measures the actual risk.
